## What it's for

Epitalon (also called Epithalon or AEDG) is a synthetic tetrapeptide derived from pineal gland research, not a conventional dietary nutrient. Its primary studied roles center on circadian and endocrine regulation: it stimulates evening melatonin synthesis and normalizes cortisol secretion, which together help restore disrupted sleep-wake cycles. It also appears to increase parasympathetic nervous system activity, supporting deeper sleep architecture, higher heart rate variability, and greater stress resilience. Reported downstream effects include more vivid dreaming, improved skin and hair vibrancy, and reduced overall sleep need. Because it is not an essential nutrient, there are no deficiency states in the clinical sense — rather, its absence simply means none of these pineal-modulatory effects are achieved.

## How to get it

Epitalon is not present in any food or drink. It is exclusively available as a synthetic research peptide in two primary forms:

- **Oral enteric-coated capsules:** The enteric coating protects the tetrapeptide from stomach acid degradation, improving intestinal absorption. However, capsule shell materials and fillers can reduce purity and bioavailability relative to powder vials.
- **Lyophilized powder vials (for reconstitution):** Reconstituted with bacteriostatic water and administered subcutaneously. This route bypasses gastrointestinal breakdown entirely, offering higher and more predictable bioavailability. Vials from reputable vendors are pure powder with no excipients.

**Salt form matters:** Source only the **acetate salt**. The trifluoroacetate (TFA) salt form carries toxic properties and can cause partial peptide misfolding. Always verify the salt form on the vendor's certificate of analysis (COA).

**Timing:** Take or administer Epitalon at least 2 hours before L-Tyrosine supplementation or high-protein meals. Large neutral amino acids compete for the same LAT1 transporter used for CNS delivery.

## Dosing

There is no established RDA, RDI, or % Daily Value for Epitalon — it is not a recognized essential nutrient. Reported research-use protocols are as follows:

- **Standard course:** 3 mg oral enteric-coated capsule daily for 14 consecutive nights, taken 1–2 hours before bed.
- **Higher-demand repletion:** 3–6 mg oral daily for 2 weeks, particularly when targeting sleep architecture or stress-resilience outcomes.
- **Cycling:** 1 month on followed by 2+ months off for maintenance; shorter 2-week cycles are used for acute repletion.
- **Titration start:** Begin at 100–200 mcg to allow any initial histamine response to subside before stepping up to a full dose.

No Tolerable Upper Intake Level has been established. These figures reflect reported experiential and limited study protocols, not regulatory guidelines.

## Risks

**Histamine response:** An initial histamine-like reaction can occur at full doses. Titrating up from 100–200 mcg typically allows this to subside.

**Form-related toxicity:** The trifluoroacetate salt form is associated with partial peptide misfolding and toxic properties. Confirm acetate salt form via the batch-specific COA before purchase or use.

**Drug and nutrient interactions:** No interactions have been studied in humans.

**Regulatory and safety status:** Epitalon is not approved as a therapeutic drug or dietary supplement by the FDA, EFSA, or equivalent bodies in most jurisdictions. Long-term human safety data are limited. It is classified and sold strictly as a research compound. Individuals with histamine intolerance, autoimmune conditions, or those on medications affecting melatonin or cortisol pathways should consult a clinician before use.
