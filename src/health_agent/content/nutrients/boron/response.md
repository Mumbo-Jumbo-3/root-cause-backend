## What it's for

Boron is a trace mineral that functions as a metabolic facilitator, helping your body more effectively utilize other vitamins and minerals. It supports bone density and cognitive function, regulates hormones by lowering excessive estrogen and SHBG while boosting testosterone levels, and enhances retention of minerals like magnesium and calcium. Boron also extends vitamin D's half-life in your system, helps remove sodium fluoride from the body, improves muscle coordination and glycogen storage, supports sleep quality, and assists adrenal gland function.

## How to get it

The best dietary sources are prunes (highest concentration), raisins, and avocados. When choosing prunes and raisins, avoid products processed with inferior oils like sunflower or canola oil. For supplementation, boron requires an insulin spike for optimal absorption, so take it in the morning with a proper meal containing carbohydrates. Without this insulin response, effects are often insignificant. Boron works synergistically with magnesium, calcium, vitamin K2, zinc, lactoferrin, and shilajit for enhanced mineral metabolism and hormone balance.

## Dosing

Take 3-6mg of elemental boron daily with breakfast or another substantial meal to ensure proper insulin response and absorption. Research studies have safely used up to 10mg daily for short periods, but 3-6mg provides optimal benefits for regular use. Consider cycling periodically rather than continuous long-term use to maintain proper mineral balance, as achieving perfect mineral equilibrium is challenging and prolonged use might displace other essential minerals.

## Risks

No major risks have been identified at recommended dosages of 3-6mg daily. Avoid exceeding 6-9mg daily for regular use. Boron has a major interaction with estrogen medications and should be balanced with other trace minerals to prevent displacement effects. Since boron affects hormone levels significantly (studies show up to 28% increase in free testosterone and 39% decrease in estrogen), monitor for hormonal changes and consult a healthcare provider if you have hormone-related conditions. Maintain adequate intake of complementary minerals like magnesium and calcium when supplementing with boron.
