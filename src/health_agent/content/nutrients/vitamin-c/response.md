# The Complete Guide to Vitamin C: Functions, Optimal Intake, and Best Products

## What Vitamin C Does for Your Health

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant that humans must obtain through diet, as our bodies cannot produce it naturally. This essential nutrient serves multiple critical functions:

**Core Health Benefits:**
- **Collagen synthesis**: Essential for healthy skin, connective tissue, bones, cartilage, and wound healing
- **Antioxidant protection**: Shields your DNA, proteins, and cells from oxidative damage
- **Immune system support**: Concentrates in white blood cells at 60 times the level found in surrounding fluids
- **Iron absorption**: Significantly enhances your body's ability to absorb iron from plant sources
- **Stress response**: Reduces cortisol levels by up to 40% during stressful periods
- **Cardiovascular health**: Improves blood vessel function and helps regulate blood pressure
- **Detoxification**: Assists in removing heavy metals like mercury and lead from your body

## How to Best Consume Vitamin C

### Food Sources (Preferred for Daily Intake)
The most bioavailable vitamin C comes from whole foods, which provide beneficial cofactors that enhance absorption:

**Top Food Sources:**
1. Acerola cherry juice
2. Camu camu powder
3. Fresh blackberries
4. Pomegranate juice
5. Kiwis
6. Orange juice
7. Bell peppers (especially red)
8. Strawberries and leafy greens

**Daily food-based goal**: 150-200mg from whole food sources

### Supplement Forms and Dosing
**When to supplement:**
- During illness or high stress
- For smokers or former smokers
- Those with frequent infections
- People taking medications that deplete vitamin C

**Supplement options:**
- **Whole food concentrates** (acerola, camu camu): Best for long-term daily use
- **Ascorbic acid or sodium ascorbate**: Effective for therapeutic doses
- **Liposomal vitamin C**: Enhanced absorption, gentler on stomach
- **Buffered forms**: Better tolerated in higher doses

**Dosing guidelines:**
- **Daily maintenance**: 250-500mg
- **Therapeutic use**: 1,000-3,000mg during illness or stress
- **Timing**: Take first thing in the morning, 1-2 hours after coffee, with meals containing sodium

### Important Precautions
- Avoid combining with high-iron supplements (increases oxidative stress)
- Don't take with B-complex vitamins at the same meal
- Limit isolated ascorbic acid to 2 months maximum
- Higher doses require adequate B6 to prevent kidney stones
- Consult healthcare providers if taking blood thinners, statins, or birth control
