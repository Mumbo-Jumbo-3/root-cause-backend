# Royal Jelly

Aliases: gelée royale

## What it's for

Royal jelly is a nutrient-dense bee-derived substance containing proteins, carbohydrates, fats, B vitamins (B1–B6, biotin, folate), and minerals including calcium, copper, iron, and potassium. Its primary bioactive compound, 10-hydroxy-2-decenoic acid (10-HDA), promotes neurogenesis and neural stem cell proliferation, supports bone formation via osteoblast differentiation, and drives mitochondrial biogenesis through increased PGC-1α expression — improving endurance and protecting against exercise-induced oxidative stress. Royal jelly also supports hormonal health by accelerating DHEA-to-testosterone conversion in men, and research in animal models points to longevity and growth benefits. It has been noted anecdotally for supporting thyroid function in conditions like Hashimoto's.

## How to get it

**Whole-food sources:**
- **Honeycomb** is the most comprehensive delivery vehicle, naturally combining royal jelly, bee pollen, propolis, and raw honey in one food. Best consumed once or twice a week, ideally post-workout.
- **Raw unheated honey** paired with royal jelly provides complementary benefits (rapid liver glycogen replenishment) without requiring significant insulin output.

**Supplement forms (ranked by bioavailability):**
- **Fresh/raw royal jelly** (refrigerated jar) — highest potency; preserves enzymes, 10-HDA, and B vitamins fully intact.
- **Raw frozen royal jelly** — equivalent potency to fresh; cold-chain packaging maintains the complete bioactive profile without processing degradation.
- **Freeze-dried capsules** — room-temperature stable and convenient; retains bioactives better than heat-dried forms and allows precise dosing.

**Bioavailability tips:**
- Sublingual administration may improve absorption by bypassing first-pass liver metabolism.
- Taking royal jelly with fat-containing or protein-rich foods appears to support bioavailability.
- Avoid heating any bee product, as this degrades enzymatic activity and heat-sensitive bioactives like 10-HDA.

## Dosing

No established RDI or RDA exists for royal jelly. Supplemental protocols in use range from **100 mg/day** (minimal daily dose) to **300–600 mg taken with a fat-containing meal**, up to **1,000 mg sublingually each morning**, or as high as **3 g/day for up to 6 months** as used in some clinical studies. For most healthy adults, 300–600 mg/day with food is a practical starting range.

**Timing:** Post-workout or morning dosing aligns with its glycogen-replenishing and anabolic recovery roles. Honeycomb as a whole-food source suits a once- or twice-weekly schedule.

**Cycling (especially for androgenic use):** For men targeting testosterone and hormonal support, cycle 3–4 weeks on followed by a 2–5 week break. If the goal is specifically lowering prolactin or boosting dopamine, 1 week of use may be sufficient. Cycling reduces risk of hormonal imbalance from sustained DHEA conversion.

## Risks

**Allergic reactions** are the most serious risk. Royal jelly is a bee product; individuals with bee allergies, asthma, or atopic conditions face risk of anaphylaxis and should avoid it or use only under medical supervision. Those with mold sensitivity, MCAS, or histamine intolerance should also exercise caution, as mycotoxin contamination is an increasing concern in bee-derived products.

**Hormonal side effects:** Royal jelly accelerates DHEA conversion, which can result in downstream estrogen production at higher or prolonged doses — potentially causing gynecomastia in men. Unsupervised high-dose or long-term continuous use is inadvisable for this reason.

**No formal tolerable upper intake level (UL)** has been established by major health authorities. Cycling is the primary risk-mitigation strategy.

**Drug and nutrient interactions:**
- **Warfarin and other blood thinners:** royal jelly has been reported to strengthen warfarin's effect and cause bleeding; combine only with INR monitoring.
- Avoid stacking with other androgenic or aphrodisiac herbs simultaneously, as additive conversion effects can become unpredictable.
- If combining with DHEA supplements, monitor hormone levels, as royal jelly accelerates DHEA metabolism and combined use may amplify androgenic or estrogenic effects.
